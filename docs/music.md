# Music

World context for music generation. Read alongside a per-track
`*.spec.md` under `assets/audio/music/`. Companion to `world.md`
(fiction) and `sound_design.md` (operational audio rules).

## Through-line

Every composed track in the game obeys these:

- **Warm, analog-feeling.** Tape warmth, tube glow, instruments you
  can hear people playing.
- **Sincere, not parody.** Psychedelic communion — never wink-at-the-
  camera "60s pastiche."
- **Exaggerated.** Everything the Realm feels, the music feels more
  of — more color, more warmth, more ache.
- **Subtle wrongness underneath.** A note off-pitch in the
  background, a sustained tail going somewhere unexpected. Always
  available; intensity varies per track. Some tracks (e.g. the main
  menu) deliberately dial it nearly to zero — see the spec.
- **No vocals (default).** Specs may explicitly opt in.

## Palette

The OST may pull from any of these — per-track specs pick the lane:

- Late-60s / early-70s psychedelic rock & pop — Jefferson Airplane,
  Grateful Dead, early Pink Floyd, *Yellow Submarine* soundtrack.
- Acid folk / freak folk — Vashti Bunyan, Tyrannosaurus Rex.
- Jam bands — Grateful Dead, Phish, Khruangbin, Goose. Extended
  grooves, exploratory soloing.
- Krautrock — Can, Neu!, early Tangerine Dream. Motorik pulse,
  hypnotic, warm electronic.
- Cosmic / spiritual jazz — Alice Coltrane, Pharoah Sanders, Sun Ra.
  Free, exploratory.
- Psychedelic dub — Lee "Scratch" Perry. Echo, space, found sound.
- Vintage analog synths — Moog, ARP, Mellotron, modular. Warm, not
  laser-clean.
- World textures — tabla, gamelan, sitar, kalimba, raga drone.
  Sparingly, never costume.
- Long-form / experimental — extended jams, drone passages, sound
  collage.

## Outside the world

Production stamps that pull the listener out of the Realm:

- Hollywood-orchestral score with timed swells.
- Modern hyper-clean pop mastering.
- Metal distortion.
- Chiptune / 8-bit.
- Synthwave neon-80s.
- Trap / 808 hip-hop.
- Lo-fi "study beats."
- Loop-intro/outro fades on a loopable track.
