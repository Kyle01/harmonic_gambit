# Theme — Main menu

**Role.** Persistent main-menu / non-combat theme. First music the
player hears — the Realm extending an open hand.

**Mood.** Minimalist, warm, welcoming. Major key. Slow (~60 BPM).
Doesn't overwhelm — a quiet doorway, not an anthem.

**Prompt body** (injected after the world context):

> A minimalist, welcoming main-menu loop in a late-60s
> psychedelic-folk register. Sparse arrangement: fingerpicked
> acoustic guitar, soft Hammond-style organ pad, gentle tambourine
> or finger-cymbal pulse — only two or three instruments sounding at
> any moment. Major key, simple resolved chord movement, no tension
> or build. ~60 BPM. Soft attacks throughout. Light room reverb only
> — no swirling plate, no phasing, no rich stereo wash. Loopable
> seamlessly: no intro, no outro, no fade. Warm and inviting — the
> Realm at its kindest, an open door. Sincere, not anthemic. No
> vocals.

**Anti-patterns.** No vocals, chant, or humming. No fade-in or
fade-out. No rock backbeat or drum kit. No orchestral swells. No
dense stereo wash. No tension or build-up — a steady welcoming bed,
not a journey. No "subtle wrongness" cues here — save those for the
map / rest / combat tracks. The menu is the Realm on its best
behavior.
