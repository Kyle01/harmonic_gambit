# Singer — character portrait

## Figure

The **Singer archetype** — a JOYOUS RADIANT figure, the sun
given voice. Arms raised in ecstatic song, mouth wide open.
NOT an elder bearded figure — this is a youthful, genderless,
beaming figure, rapt in song.

Features:

- **A young, beardless, genderless face**, mouth wide open
  in full-throated song.
- **Long flowing rose or gold hair** radiating outward.
- **Arms raised high above the head**, palms open, fingers
  spread in rapture.
- **A loose sleeveless moss-green tunic** or bare-shouldered
  drape.
- **A small crown of sunflowers / coral blossoms** on the
  head.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (voice as instrument)

The Singer's instrument is their VOICE. Render it
symbolically:

- **Bold-outlined sound-wave ripples** radiating from the
  open mouth outward across the canvas.
- **Small stylized music-note shapes** floating in the air
  around the figure.
- NO microphone, NO megaphone, NO held object — the voice
  itself, visualized as radiating waves and notes, is the
  instrument.

## Background

Flat gold-yellow field with a LARGE GOLDEN SUN DISC behind
the figure's head, ray-spikes radiating outward to fill the
canvas. Small sunflower silhouettes along the bottom edge.
Ornate tarot-style frame.

## No text, no numbers

No card number, title, label, writing, or numerals anywhere.
