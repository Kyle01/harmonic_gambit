# Accordion — character portrait

## Figure

The **Accordion archetype** — a wandering JESTER-FOOL, the
village dance-caller stepping off a cliff into his own
song. Whimsical, smiling, light-footed.

Features:

- **A bushy grey handlebar mustache**, wide and curling
  upward at the ends (signature feature).
- **A round flat cap** (newsboy / peasant) in coral.
- **A wide easy smile**.
- **Short grey hair** visible under the cap.
- **Rosy weather-worn cheeks**, crow's-feet at the eyes.
- **A moss-green vest** over a loose gold-yellow shirt with
  rolled sleeves.
- **One leg raised mid-step** (walking / dancing posture).
- **A small single coral rose or flower** tucked into the
  cap or vest.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (an accordion)

An **accordion** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-
outlined:

- **Expanding pleated bellows** in the center (rows of
  folded pleats — the unmistakable identifier).
- **A keyboard or button panel** on one side (rows of small
  round buttons).
- **A bass-button panel** on the other side.
- The accordion body is **box-shaped on each end**, bellows
  stretched between.

Held across the chest, bellows wide, hands playing.

## Background

Flat gold-yellow field with a CLIFF-EDGE below the figure's
feet and distant small rolling mountains beyond. A bright
sun disc in one corner. A small stylized white dog at the
figure's heel. Ornate tarot-style frame.
