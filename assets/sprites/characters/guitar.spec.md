# Guitar — character portrait

## Figure

The **Guitar archetype** — a bearded mythic elder musician,
rendered flat and symbolic. A wandering prophet of song.

Features (all drawn flat, with bold black outlines, no
realistic rendering):

- **Long flat grey beard** reaching to the chest — a single
  solid shape with a bold outline, no strand detail.
- **Weathered flat face** with a few bold outline lines
  suggesting age (brow, cheek, mouth lines) — symbolic, not
  rendered.
- **A broad-brim hat** or simple hood as a single flat shape
  (gold-yellow or moss-green).
- **Long flat grey hair** as solid shape-locks past the
  shoulders.

Clothes: a flat robe or long loose coat in moss-green or
gold-yellow with coral trim, folds as simple outlined shape-
bands.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (a guitar MUST be clearly visible)

An **acoustic guitar** is the defining symbol of this card.
It MUST appear prominently and unmistakably. The guitar
identifies this character — if it is missing or unclear,
the image has failed.

Rendering:

- **The guitar body** (the big rounded hourglass wooden
  shape) is the most important element — draw it large,
  clearly, rendered flat with a bold outline.
- **A large round sound-hole** in the center of the guitar
  body. The sound-hole is the single shape that makes this
  unambiguously a guitar (not a lute, banjo, or dulcimer).
- **Six strings** running the length of the neck.
- **A fretted neck** with a headstock at the top.

Placement: either held diagonally across the figure's
chest (body lower-right, neck going up across the left
shoulder), OR floating behind the figure as a large
tarot-symbolic emblem (body visible, neck visible). Either
way, the body of the guitar and the round sound-hole must
be clearly readable in the final image.

## Background

Flat warm solid color field (moss-green or gold-yellow) with
simple Rider-Waite-style symbolic motifs — a sun disc or
crescent moon in one corner, small scattered stars, a
geometric horizon line. All flat, bold-outlined, symbolic.
No rendered landscape. A decorative border frame around the
edges is welcome (tarot-card register).

## No text, no numbers

- **No tarot card number** at the top of the card.
- **No title or roman numeral** at the bottom.
- **No letters, no words, no name plaque, no banner, no
  label.** Nothing written or numbered anywhere in the
  image — the card is unlabeled.
