# Bass — character portrait

## Figure

The **Bass archetype** — a grounded, heavy, low-end menace.
A horned elder figure, muscular and chained to the earth.

Features:

- **Bald head** with two SHORT BLUNT HORNS curving forward
  from the temples (small, stylized, ram-like — not demonic
  spikes).
- **Heavy bushy grey mutton-chops sideburns** connecting to
  a thick grey mustache, NO chin beard.
- **Deep-set heavy brow**, weathered leathery face, dark
  shadowed eyes.
- **Broad bare shoulders** or a sleeveless black leather
  vest with silver studs.
- **A heavy iron chain** draped across the chest.
- **A silver hoop earring** in one ear.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (an electric bass guitar)

An **electric bass guitar** is the defining symbol. Draw
large, flat, bold-outlined:

- **A solid slab body** (elongated teardrop / stratocaster
  shape) in coral — NO sound-hole (key mark, distinguishes
  from acoustic guitar).
- **Four thick strings** along the neck.
- **A long fretted neck** with a headstock and tuning pegs.

Held diagonally across the figure's chest, body lower-right,
neck angled up across the left shoulder.

## Background

Flat deep coral or dark-moss-green field. Two small upright
flame-torches at the figure's shoulders. A heavy chain
draped across the bottom edge. Ornate tarot-style frame.

## No text, no numbers

No card number, title, label, writing, or numerals anywhere.
