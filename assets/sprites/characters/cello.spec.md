# Cello — character portrait

## Figure

The **Cello archetype** — a VEILED PRIESTESS, melancholy and
mystical. Seated between two dark pillars, gazing outward
with quiet sorrow. NOT a bearded man — this is a female
figure, silver-haired, wise, still.

Features:

- **A female figure** with long silver-grey hair flowing
  past the shoulders.
- **A thin silver crescent-moon crown** on the forehead (two
  upward horns of a crescent moon).
- **A long flowing veil** in moss-green draped from the
  crown, framing the face.
- **A deep blue-moss-green robe** with coral accents at the
  collar.
- **A calm melancholy expression**, eyes looking toward the
  viewer with quiet sadness.
- **Seated posture**, upright and still.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (a cello)

A **cello** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-outlined:

- **A large curved wooden body** (violin-shaped, cello-
  sized — roughly torso-size).
- **Two S-shaped F-holes** on the body (violin-family
  marker, distinguishes from guitar).
- **A long neck** rising up beside the figure.
- **An ornate scroll** at the top of the neck.
- **Four strings** along the neck.
- **A bow** in one hand, angled across the strings.

The cello sits vertically in front of the figure, body at
chest level, neck extending up past the head.

## Background

Flat deep-moss-green field with TWO TALL BLACK PILLARS
flanking the figure — one with a SUN motif at the top, the
other with a MOON motif at the top. Small scattered stars.
Ornate tarot-style frame.
