# DJ — character portrait

## Figure

The **DJ archetype** — a cosmic GRIOT at the turning wheel
of fate. The block-party shaman spinning time forward and
back. Hands resting on a great wheel of records.

Features:

- **A grey-white afro** (large, round, full).
- **A thick grey goatee beard**.
- **Round yellow-tinted sunglasses**.
- **A coral dashiki or patterned tunic** with geometric
  gold-yellow motifs across the chest.
- **A weathered dark-complected face** with deep lines.
- **Headphones around the neck**.
- **Both hands resting on a large central wheel**, palms
  touching its rim.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (turntables / vinyl records)

**A large cosmic turning-wheel of vinyl records** is the
defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-outlined:

- **A LARGE CENTRAL WHEEL** filling the middle of the
  canvas — concentric grooved black circles (vinyl records
  stacked / spinning on a platter).
- **A small round center hole** in each record.
- The figure's hands rest on the rim of the wheel.
- Optionally: a second small record floating to one side.

Black circles with visible groove lines = unambiguous vinyl.

## Background

Flat gold-yellow field with constellation motifs in the four
corners — simple bold-outlined symbolic creatures (small
stylized bull, lion, eagle, and star figures, one per
corner). Concentric ring ripples emanating from the central
wheel. Ornate tarot-style frame.

## No text, no numbers

No card number, title, label, writing, or numerals anywhere.
