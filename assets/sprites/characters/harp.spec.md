# Harp — character portrait

This card is **TEMPERANCE** from the Rider-Waite tarot, re-
cast as a harp player. Use the iconic Temperance composition:
a winged angelic androgynous figure with a halo, robes
flowing, one foot on land and one in water, music passing
between the hands like water between vessels. Match
TEMPERANCE's symbolic register exactly — flat, hieratic,
bold-outlined.

## Figure

An **ANGELIC ANDROGYNOUS FIGURE** — beardless, serene,
haloed, winged. NOT a bearded elder. NOT a wizard. NOT a
hermit. NOT a wanderer-prophet. NOT a man with a beard.

Features:

- **A young-to-middle-aged androgynous face**, beardless,
  serene, eyes calmly closed or gently lowered.
- **Long flowing white or silver hair**.
- **A golden circle-halo** around the head.
- **Two small white angel-wings** emerging from the
  shoulders.
- **Long flowing moss-green and gold-yellow robes** with
  coral trim.
- **Both hands raised**, one gently plucking the harp
  strings, the other guiding the flow.
- **One foot bare on land**, one foot touching water (if
  visible).

## Instrument — CRITICAL (a harp)

A **harp** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-outlined:

- **A tall triangular frame** — a curved top beam meeting a
  vertical pillar at a sharp point (the unmistakable harp
  triangle silhouette).
- **Many parallel vertical strings** filling the inside of
  the triangle (15–20+ visible — the dense parallel lines
  are the identifier).
- **An ornate scrolled top** where beam meets pillar.
- **A soundboard base** at the bottom.

The harp stands vertically in front of the figure, triangle
prominent, the strings read like flowing water.

## Background

Flat moss-green field. A small path winds from the figure's
feet toward a distant rising sun at the horizon. Scattered
small stars. Flowing bold-outlined water-ripples at the
base. Ornate tarot-style frame.

## No text, no numbers

No card number, title, label, writing, or numerals anywhere.
