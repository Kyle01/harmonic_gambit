# Upright Bass — character portrait

## Figure

The **Upright Bass archetype** — a thoughtful BEATNIK
contemplative, the thinking-man's low-end prophet. Head-
tilted, suspended mid-thought.

Features:

- **A thin pointed grey goatee** (small, chin-only).
- **A black beret** tilted on the head.
- **Round wire-frame reading glasses**.
- **Short grey hair** visible under the beret.
- **A black turtleneck sweater** (minimal, not ornamented).
- **A weathered thoughtful face**, head tilted noticeably to
  one side as if held upside-down in thought.
- **A faint golden halo** around the head.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (an upright bass)

An **upright bass** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-
outlined:

- **A large curved wooden body** (violin-shaped but massive,
  much bigger than the figure).
- **Two S-shaped F-holes** on the body (violin-family
  marker, distinguishes from guitar).
- **A long wooden neck** extending above the figure's head.
- **An ornate scroll** at the very top of the neck.
- **Four thick strings** visible along the neck.

The bass stands vertically beside/behind the figure, neck
extending up past the head.

## Background

Flat gold-yellow field with a simple T-shaped tree or
gallows-post silhouette behind the figure (thin, stylized,
not menacing — a meditative cross-shape). A small halo
behind the head. Scattered stars. Ornate tarot-style frame.
