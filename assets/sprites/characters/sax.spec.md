# Sax — character portrait

This card is **THE STAR** from the Rider-Waite tarot, re-cast
as a saxophone player. Use the iconic Star composition: a
youthful figure in a flowing moss-green robe kneeling at the
edge of a pool under one giant eight-pointed star and seven
small stars, music pouring from the instrument like water.
Match THE STAR's symbolic register — flat, hieratic, bold-
outlined.

## Figure

A **youthful nymph archetype** kneeling beside the pool —
slender, androgynous, beardless. Drawn as a hieratic
symbolic figure: flowing decorative curves rather than
realistic anatomy, ornate and stylized in the manner of an
illuminated panel.

Features:

- **A young, gentle, beardless face**, eyes half-closed in
  calm concentration.
- **Long flowing wavy hair** down the back, drawn as
  decorative ornamental curves, rose or coral tinted.
- **A flowing moss-green robe / loose tunic** draped over
  the body, gathered at the waist with a coral sash. The
  figure is fully clothed.
- **A small gold-star circlet** on the forehead.
- **Kneeling pose**, one knee on the ground, one raised —
  the composition leans into curving organic line.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (a saxophone)

A **saxophone** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-
outlined:

- **A curved golden-brass body** — distinctive J-curve
  silhouette, NOT straight.
- **A wide flared bell** at the bottom.
- **Rows of round keys / pads** along the body (the flat
  circles are the key identifier).
- **A curved mouthpiece and neck** at the top.

The figure tilts the sax so its bell points down toward a
pool of water — music flowing from the bell as a stream of
sound-wave ripples pouring into the pool.

## Background

Flat deep moss-green or indigo night sky with ONE LARGE
EIGHT-POINTED STAR directly above the figure's head and
seven smaller stars scattered around. A small pool of water
at the figure's feet, music-ripples on its surface. Ornate
tarot-style frame.
