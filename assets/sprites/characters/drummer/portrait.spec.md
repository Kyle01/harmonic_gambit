# Drummer — character portrait

## Figure

The **Drummer archetype** — a shamanic active magician,
channeling rhythm. ARMS RAISED, one pointing up, one pointing
down (classic magician pose).

Features:

- **Long thick grey dreadlocks** radiating outward like
  sun-rays.
- **A thick grey goatee beard**, no mustache.
- **A weathered sun-bronzed face** with coral tribal-paint
  markings (simple dots or stripes) across the cheeks.
- **Bare muscular arms** raised wide — one hand up holding a
  drumstick toward the sky, the other hand down holding a
  drumstick toward the ground.
- **A loose moss-green sleeveless tunic**, beaded leather
  wristbands.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (drums)

**Drums** are the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-outlined:

- **A large round snare drum** at the figure's waist, flat
  circle with tension lugs visible.
- **A large round bass drum** silhouetted behind the figure.
- **A round cymbal disc** floating above one shoulder.
- **Two drumsticks** held up / down in the raised hands.

Circles with stretched drum-head surfaces — unambiguously
drums, not strings.

## Background

Flat gold-yellow field with a large LEMNISCATE / INFINITY
SYMBOL ∞ floating horizontally above the figure's head. Sun-
burst rays radiating from behind. Small scattered stars.
Ornate tarot-style frame.
