# Keyboard — character portrait

## Figure

The **Keyboard archetype** — a PRIESTLY SCHOLAR elder,
seated on a throne of music, hand raised in blessing.
Formal, hieratic, dignified.

Features:

- **A full flowing white beard** reaching the chest.
- **A triple-tiered crown or tall mitre-style headdress** in
  gold-yellow with coral trim (priestly, not kingly).
- **Wild white hair** visible at the sides.
- **Heavy dark eyebrows** above small round spectacles.
- **Formal layered priestly robes** — outer coral robe over
  gold-yellow under-robe, with a dark-moss-green sash.
- **Seated posture**, one hand raised palm-outward in
  blessing/greeting gesture.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (a piano keyboard)

A **piano keyboard** is the defining symbol. Draw flat,
bold-outlined:

- **A horizontal strip of alternating black-and-white keys**
  stretching across the lower third of the canvas (the
  striped black/white pattern is the unmistakable mark).
- At least 8–12 keys visible, each clearly outlined.
- The keyboard rests on an altar or lectern in front of the
  seated figure.

## Background

Flat moss-green field with TWO TALL PILLARS flanking the
figure (one to each side) — simple bold-outlined columns in
gold-yellow. Concentric rings behind the head like a halo.
Small scattered stars. Ornate tarot-style frame.

No card number, title, label, writing, or numerals anywhere.
