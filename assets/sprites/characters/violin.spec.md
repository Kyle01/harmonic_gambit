# Violin — character portrait

This card is **THE MOON** from the Rider-Waite tarot, re-cast
as a violin player. Use the iconic Moon composition: night
scene under a giant crescent moon, two stylized wolves /
dogs howling at the moon at the lower corners, a winding
path leading toward a distant tower. Match THE MOON's
symbolic register exactly — flat, hieratic, bold-outlined.

## Figure

A **MYSTERIOUS GYPSY FIDDLER** at night — road-wise, fierce,
strange. Playing the violin beneath the moon.

Features:

- **A long grey beard** with a slightly pointed end.
- **A red or coral headscarf** tied around the head, knotted
  at the side.
- **Long grey hair** partly covered by the scarf.
- **A single silver hoop earring**.
- **A moss-green vest with embroidered coral trim** over a
  loose gold-yellow shirt.
- **A weathered bronze-complected face** with sharp angular
  features, mysterious expression.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (a violin)

A **violin** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-
outlined:

- **A small curved wooden body** (violin-shaped, fits tucked
  under the chin).
- **Two S-shaped F-holes** on the body (violin-family
  marker, distinguishes from guitar).
- **A short neck** with fingerboard.
- **An ornate scroll** at the top of the neck.
- **Four strings** along the neck.
- **A bow** held in the other hand, drawn across the
  strings.

Held tucked under the chin, body on the shoulder, bow drawn
across.

## Background

Flat deep-indigo or dark-moss-green night sky with a LARGE
CRESCENT MOON dominating the upper portion of the canvas.
TWO STYLIZED WOLVES OR DOGS silhouetted at the lower corners,
howling upward at the moon. A single tall stylized tower
silhouette in the far distance. Scattered stars. Ornate
tarot-style frame.
