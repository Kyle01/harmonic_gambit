# Mariachi — band card portrait

## RULE 1: NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO LABELS, NO BANNER, NO PLAQUE

Nothing written or printed anywhere in the image. No tarot title, no
roman numeral, no name banner, no card-foot label. Empty borders.

## RULE 2: A FIVE-PIECE MARIACHI ARC

Five flat dark-blue silhouette figures arranged in a festive shallow
arc. Two vocalists share the front, with strings flanking and behind:

1. **Front-center-left: a vocalist at a standing microphone.** Round
   mic ball at mouth height, short pale-blue mic stand rising to
   chest height. Leaning slightly forward into the mic.
2. **Front-center-right: a second vocalist at a second standing
   microphone.** Mirrored to the first vocalist, sharing harmonies.
   The two mic stands are close together, almost touching.
3. **Stage-left: a violinist.** Standing, violin tucked under the
   chin, bow raised mid-stroke.
4. **Back-left: an acoustic-guitar player.** Standing, holding an
   acoustic guitar with a rounded hollow body slung at chest height,
   both hands on it.
5. **Back-right: a second acoustic-guitar player.** Mirrored stance
   to the first guitarist, rounded hollow-body guitar slung at chest
   height.

The arrangement reads as a close-packed festive band, two vocalists
front-and-center, the strings fanning out behind.

## NOT THESE

This is **NOT a parade column**, **NOT a marching band**. There is
**NO saxophone, NO trumpet, NO horn of any kind** in this card.
There is **no accordion** (this is mariachi, not norteño), **no drum
kit**, **no electric instruments**. The figures do **NOT wear charro
hats** that obscure silhouettes — keep heads plain round silhouettes.

## Figure pose

All five figures in flat dark-blue silhouette, no facial features, no
clothing detail. The two vocalists upright at their mics; the violinist
mid-stroke; both guitarists planted, hands on their instruments.

## Background

A radial spotlight halo behind the five figures: pale ice-blue at the
center fading to deep navy at the edges. Around it, a deep navy field
with a small mid-blue crescent moon and a few pale-blue stars
scattered. No foreground grass, no plants, no foliage — just the
figures, their instruments, and the spotlight.

A simple deep-navy tarot border with a thin pale-blue inner line frames
the card. The border is empty — no text, no numerals, no banner.

## Palette — STRICT MONOCHROMATIC BLUE

Every shape is one of: deep navy, cobalt / mid-blue, or pale ice-blue.
No warm hues — no gold, green, rose, coral, or brown anywhere.
