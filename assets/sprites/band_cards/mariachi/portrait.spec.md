# Mariachi — band card portrait

## Figure

A single **tall charro mariachi frontman, sombrero on his head,
holding an acoustic guitar mid-strum**, frontal and centered,
filling the canvas. Single figure only.

Features (all flat dark-blue silhouette, no facial features, no
clothing detail beyond outline):

- **A BROAD-BRIM SOMBRERO** as a single bold flat shape on the
  head — wide curved brim, tall round crown. Render large and
  unmistakable.
- **A wide muscular silhouette** standing tall and frontal.
- **A long fitted CHARRO JACKET** silhouette with vertical bands
  of decorative outline-trim down the sides.
- **A small bowtie shape** at the throat.
- **High-waisted trousers** with narrow legs ending in simple
  boots.
- **The acoustic guitar held diagonally across the chest**, both
  hands on it.

## Instrument — CRITICAL (acoustic guitar)

An **acoustic guitar** is the defining symbol. Draw flat, bold-
outlined:

- **A LARGE ROUNDED HOURGLASS WOODEN BODY** — the big rounded
  hourglass shape that distinguishes acoustic from electric. This
  is not a slim solid body.
- **A LARGE ROUND SOUND-HOLE** in the center of the body. The
  sound-hole is the single shape that makes this unambiguously an
  acoustic guitar.
- **Six strings** running the length of the neck.
- **A fretted neck** with a headstock at the top.

Held diagonally across the figure's chest — body lower-right,
neck going up across the left shoulder.

## Background

A radial spotlight halo behind the figure: pale ice-blue at the
center fading to deep navy at the edges. A deep navy field with a
small mid-blue crescent moon and scattered pale-blue stars across
the upper field. No foreground props, no scenery, no foliage.

A simple deep-navy tarot-style border with a thin pale-blue inner
line frames the card. The border is empty.

## Palette — STRICT MONOCHROMATIC BLUE

Every shape is one of: deep navy, cobalt / mid-blue, or pale
ice-blue. No warm hues anywhere.

## No text, no numbers

No card number, title, label, writing, or numerals anywhere.
