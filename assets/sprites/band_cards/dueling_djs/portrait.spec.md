# Dueling DJs — band card portrait

## RULE 1: NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO LABELS, NO BANNER, NO PLAQUE

Nothing written or printed anywhere in the image. No tarot title, no
roman numeral, no name banner, no card-foot label. Empty borders.

## RULE 2: TWO DJs FACING EACH OTHER ACROSS TURNTABLE DECKS

Two flat dark-blue silhouette figures, mirrored, standing behind their
own turntable rigs:

1. **Stage-left DJ.** Standing behind a low rectangular turntable
   table. On the table: two round flat circles (the platters) side by
   side, each with a small spindle dot at center. The figure leans in,
   one hand low over the left platter.
2. **Stage-right DJ.** Mirror image of the left DJ. Standing behind
   their own identical turntable rig with two round platters, leaning
   in, one hand on the right platter.

The two rigs face each other across the spotlight, suggesting a duel
or back-and-forth set. Headphones on each DJ — a thin band over the
silhouette's head with pads on each ear — are subtly suggested.

## NOT THESE

These are **NOT laptop DJs** — they stand behind real vinyl turntable
rigs with visible round platters. They are **NOT facing the viewer**
straight on — they angle slightly toward each other. There is no
crowd, no dance floor, no equalizer-bar visualization.

## Figure pose

Both figures in flat dark-blue silhouette, no facial features, no
clothing detail. Postures are mirrored — each leaning slightly toward
the other across the decks.

## Background

A radial spotlight halo behind the two DJs: pale ice-blue at the
center fading to deep navy at the edges. The halo is centered between
them, lighting both. Around it, a deep navy field with a small mid-blue
crescent moon and a few pale-blue stars scattered. No foreground
grass, no plants, no foliage — just the two figures, their decks, and
the spotlight.

A simple deep-navy tarot border with a thin pale-blue inner line frames
the card. The border is empty — no text, no numerals, no banner.

## Palette — STRICT MONOCHROMATIC BLUE

Every shape is one of: deep navy, cobalt / mid-blue, or pale ice-blue.
No warm hues — no gold, green, rose, coral, or brown anywhere.
