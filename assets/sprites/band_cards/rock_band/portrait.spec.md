# Rock Band — band card portrait

## RULE 1: NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO LABELS, NO BANNER, NO PLAQUE

Nothing written or printed anywhere in the image. No tarot title, no
roman numeral, no name banner, no card-foot label. Empty borders.

## RULE 2: A FOUR-PIECE ROCK BAND ON STAGE

Four flat dark-blue silhouette figures arranged across the frame,
together. Reading left to right:

1. **Stage-left: an electric-guitar player.** Holding an electric guitar
   slung at hip height — a slim solid body with a long neck angled
   downward. Stance leaning forward.
2. **Center-left: a vocalist at a vintage standing microphone.** Same
   register as a 1950s lounge singer. Round mic ball at mouth height,
   short pale-blue mic stand rising to chest height of the figure.
3. **Center-right: a bass player.** Holding an electric bass guitar,
   thicker body and shorter neck than the lead guitar, slung lower at
   waist. Stance steady, planted.
4. **Stage-right (back): a drummer behind a small drum kit.** Only the
   silhouette of the drummer's torso and arms is visible above a
   simplified kit — one bass drum, one snare, one suspended cymbal on
   a stand.

The figures stand close together, suggesting a band on a small stage.

## NOT THESE

Not a marching column. Not a parade. Not a single hero. The vocalist
is **NOT holding a guitar**. The guitar player is **NOT singing into a
microphone**. The drummer is **NOT standing in front** — they are
upstage behind the kit. There is no audience, no stage lights besides
the single spotlight halo.

## Figure pose

All four figures in flat dark-blue silhouette, no facial features, no
clothing detail. Postures are subtly varied — guitar leaning, vocalist
upright, bass planted, drummer's arms raised mid-strike.

## Background

A radial spotlight halo behind the band: pale ice-blue at the center
fading to deep navy at the edges. Around it, a deep navy field with a
small mid-blue crescent moon and a few pale-blue stars scattered. No
foreground grass, no plants, no foliage — just the four figures, their
instruments, and the spotlight.

A simple deep-navy tarot border with a thin pale-blue inner line frames
the card. The border is empty — no text, no numerals, no banner.

## Palette — STRICT MONOCHROMATIC BLUE

Every shape is one of: deep navy, cobalt / mid-blue, or pale ice-blue.
No warm hues — no gold, green, rose, coral, or brown anywhere.
