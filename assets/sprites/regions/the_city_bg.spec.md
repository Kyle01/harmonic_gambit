# The City — region background

A vast art-nouveau dream-city of impossibly stacked ivory and
cream architecture rises from the foreground into the upper sky.
A wide procession-street enters the frame at the bottom-center
and curves gently upward between the buildings, populated with
silhouetted dreamers and bright market-awnings, vanishing into
a glowing pastel-warm horizon between distant spires.

This is the **social region** — a spiral of dreamers stacked
sky-high. Markets, parlors, ten-thousand small kindnesses of
strangers in a hat. Bright, busy, generous; warm but cooler in
register than the introduction — the off-white architecture
takes the color cues from the sky.

## The path (the procession-street)

A wide ivory-cobblestone street, worn smooth, enters the frame
at the bottom-center and curves gently upward. Edges are
dappled rose-gold from sunset light. Bright market-awnings in
**every pastel hue** flank the street on both sides at head-height
— rose, lavender, mint, peach, cornflower, butter-yellow.
Silhouetted dreamers (small figures, no facial detail) drift
along the street in groups of two and three. Where the
introduction uses sunflowers and grass, this scene uses **market
awnings, lantern-strings, and ornamental ironwork** flanking the
trail.

## Around the path (the city)

Past the awnings: an impossibly stacked skyline of ivory and
cream architecture rises in tiers. Mucha-curve archways, Klimt-gold
spires with floral filigree, Yellow-Submarine pastel facades with
bulbous balconies. Buildings bend gently — none are perfectly
vertical. They lean toward each other across the street like
trees in a canopy. Towers stack on top of towers in dream-logic
ways, each tier set back from the one below. Strings of lanterns
and banners run between rooftops.

Sky takes the upper third (the buildings own the upper-middle):
a pastel-warm gradient running **peach into rose into pale
lavender**, warming to a small gold band right at the horizon
glow. Cornflower sparingly above the highest spires for contrast.
Soft pastel cloud forms with gentle organic spirals weave between
the highest towers.

## Psychedelic register

The psychedelia lives in **scale, stacking, and curve**, not
surface decoration:

- Buildings stacked impossibly tall, leaning toward each other.
- Spires curve and bend gently — none are perfectly vertical.
- Glow blooms around lanterns, awning-tops, and the brightest
  windows like an overexposed photograph.
- Soft melting cloud silhouettes weaving between towers.
- The procession-street curves upward into the architecture in a
  way that defies perspective — dream-logic depth.
- Pastel rainbow trim everywhere: lavender shutters, rose
  cornices, mint domes, peach awnings.
- The whole scene tilts a few degrees toward dream-logic — scale
  is wrong in a way that feels generous and welcoming, not
  threatening.

Heinz Edelmann's *Yellow Submarine* Pepperland skyline is the
direct lineage. Alphonse Mucha's curving ornamental linework.
Gustav Klimt's gold filigree and pattern density. Peter Max's
warm-saturated cosmic-pop sky. Moebius's clean dream-logic
architecture. Roger Dean's biomorphic stacked-tier language
(take the language, leave the prog-fantasy connotation).

## Composition

- 16:9 aspect (matches the 1280×720 logical viewport at category
  defaults: 400×225 source, scaled up at runtime via Nearest
  filter).
- **Street as leading line.** Foreground bottom-center → midground
  curve between awnings → distant horizon glow between spires.
  The street starts wide in the foreground and tapers as it
  recedes into the city.
- **Open upper-right corner.** A single floating button will sit
  in the top-right of the runtime UI — leave that area visually
  quiet (sky, soft cloud forms only — no spire intrusion).
- 16-bit pixel art register: limited shading bands per surface,
  soft dithering in the gradients, anti-aliased silhouettes,
  single-color outlines on hero shapes (foreground awnings,
  building edges, lanterns, dreamer silhouettes).
- The image is full-bleed, no border, no baked-in lettering.
