# The Expanse — region background

A vast unbroken pasture of wind-rippled tall grasses stretches
to a flat low horizon under an enormous cornflower-blue sky. **No
road, no path, no trail, no destination in frame.** The composition
is dominated by negative space — the sky owns two-thirds of the
image, the empty grassland the lower third. A single isolated
silhouette far in the middle distance anchors the eye but does
not invite approach.

This is the **open region** — an open sky, the Realm at its
quietest, where a chord can carry a long way. Reverent, generous,
slightly lonely. The wide-open feeling of unbroken pasture
endlessness — the kind of emptiness that makes a person feel
small in a peaceful way.

## The foreground (grass, no path)

The lower third of the frame is **wind-rippled tall grasses** in
soft sage-green and warm gold-yellow, leaning gently in the same
direction as if a long slow wind is moving across the whole field.
A few oversized wildflowers — wild lavender, chamomile, single
poppy heads — scatter sparingly through the grass at head-height,
leaning with the wind. The grass extends unbroken to the flat
horizon. **There is absolutely no road, no path, no trail, no
fence, no track of any kind.** The eye crosses the grass freely
to the horizon.

## The middle distance (a single anchor)

Far away, near the horizon line, a single isolated silhouette
anchors the eye. Choose **one** of the following — not all of
them — to keep the composition spare:

- A leaning wooden weather-shrine or shepherd's marker, tilted
  slightly, standing alone.
- A small stand of three or four cypress-like trees clustered
  together in the deep distance.
- A small Roger-Dean-style floating monolith hanging low in the
  sky above the horizon, casting a soft warm shadow on the
  grass below.

Whichever is chosen, it is **small in frame** and **far away**.
It does not dominate. The emptiness around it does.

## The sky (the dominant element)

The upper two-thirds of the frame is sky. A pastel gradient
running **cornflower-blue at the top into pale azure into warm
gold at the horizon line**. A low oversized warm sun sits on
the horizon — not setting, just *low*, a beat too large for
realism. Soft pastel cloud forms drift in slow horizontal
streaks, melting silhouettes, never busy. The sky is the
emotional weight of the image; it should feel enormous.

## Psychedelic register

The psychedelia here lives in **scale, emptiness, and the soft
oversized sun**, not surface decoration:

- The sun is a beat too large; it sits low, warm, generous.
- The sky bends gently at the upper edges of frame — a subtle
  horizon curve.
- Glow blooms around the sun and the brightest grass-tops like
  an overexposed photograph.
- Soft melting cloud silhouettes drift in slow horizontal
  streaks.
- The single distant anchor is at slightly the wrong scale —
  too small, somehow, against the enormousness of the sky.
- The whole scene tilts a few degrees toward dream-logic —
  *unbroken pasture endlessness* in a way that feels reverent
  and slightly lonely, never threatening.

Heinz Edelmann's *Yellow Submarine* empty-sea vistas are the
direct lineage. Peter Max's warm-saturated cosmic-pop sky.
Moebius's clean dream-logic horizon and big empty desert
panels. Roger Dean's biomorphic floating-island language —
sparingly, only if chosen as the single anchor.

## Composition

- 16:9 aspect (matches the 1280×720 logical viewport at category
  defaults: 400×225 source, scaled up at runtime via Nearest
  filter).
- **Sky-dominant**, not path-led. The upper two-thirds is sky;
  the lower third is grass. A flat low horizon line cuts across
  the image around the lower-third mark.
- **Open upper-right corner.** A single floating button will sit
  in the top-right of the runtime UI — leave that area visually
  quiet (sky, soft cloud forms only).
- 16-bit pixel art register: limited shading bands per surface,
  soft dithering in the gradients, anti-aliased silhouettes,
  single-color outlines on hero shapes (foreground grass clumps,
  the single distant anchor, the sun disc).
- The image is full-bleed, no border, no baked-in lettering.
