# The Introduction — region background

A winding dirt path begins in the foreground (lower-center,
near the viewer) and curves back through the picture, weaving
deeper and deeper into a yellow-saturated dreamscape toward a
glowing horizon. The path is the spine of the composition —
the eye follows it *into* the scene, and every other element
arranges itself around the path.

This is the **start of a journey** through the Realm. Where
the Realm first sings to you. Soft hands, gentle questions,
the occasional song. Sincerely blissful, gentle, generous —
happy and exciting in the way the first scene of an animated
journey film feels.

## The path

The dirt path is wide enough for a traveler, worn smooth, and
its edges are dappled gold-yellow. It enters the frame at the
bottom-center and curves once or twice as it recedes, climbing
gently over swelling hills until it disappears into the
horizon glow. Tall yellow flowers — sunflowers, mustard,
goldenrod — lean over the trail from both sides, taller than
realism allows, head-height as if the traveler is the small
one. Where the main menu uses pine trees and rocky boulders
flanking its road, this scene uses oversized flowers,
swaying grasses, and biomorphic blooms.

## Around the path

Past the flowers: rolling soft hills, gentle curves,
flat-color fields in chartreuse and gold and warm green-yellow.
Hills swell and breathe like a Yellow-Submarine vista. The
horizon glows where a low warm sun sits a beat too large; a
faint second sun or moon hangs nearby as a soft disc. The
land bends gently upward at the edges of frame — a subtle
horizon curve.

Sky takes the upper half: a pastel-warm gradient running
**yellow into gold into chartreuse**, warming to a small
rose-coral band right at the horizon glow. Cornflower
sparingly in the upper sky for contrast. Soft pastel cloud
forms with gentle organic spirals and melting silhouettes.

## Psychedelic register

The psychedelia lives in **light and scale and curve**, not
surface decoration:

- Flowers larger than houses, head-height over the path.
- The horizon bends gently upward; the land breathes.
- Glow blooms around the sun and the brightest flower-tops
  like an overexposed photograph.
- Soft melting cloud silhouettes; gentle organic spirals in
  the sky.
- Two sun-discs overlapping, one a beat larger than the other.
- The whole scene tilts a few degrees toward dream-logic —
  scale is wrong in a way that feels generous, not threatening.

Heinz Edelmann's *Yellow Submarine* journey vistas are the
direct lineage — saturated flat-color fields, winding road
through rolling hills, oversized flora, surreal proportions.
Peter Max's warm-saturated cosmic-pop sky. Moebius's clean
line and dream-logic horizon. Roger Dean's biomorphic
landscape language (curving hills, oversized blooms — take
the language, leave the prog-fantasy connotation).

## Composition

- 16:9 aspect (matches the 1280×720 logical viewport at
  category defaults: 400×225 source, scaled up at runtime via
  Nearest filter).
- **Path as leading line.** Foreground bottom-center →
  midground curve → distant horizon glow. The path starts
  large in the foreground and tapers as it recedes.
- **Open upper-right corner.** A single floating button will
  sit in the top-right of the runtime UI — leave that area
  visually quiet (sky, soft glow).
- 16-bit pixel art register: limited shading bands per
  surface, soft dithering in the gradients, anti-aliased
  silhouettes, single-color outlines on hero shapes
  (foreground flowers, path edges).
- The image is full-bleed, no border, no baked-in lettering.
