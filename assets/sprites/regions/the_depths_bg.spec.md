# The Depths — region background

A winding path descends from the foreground into a vast subterranean
cavern bathed in violet bioluminescence. The path enters the frame
at the bottom-center and curves down between glowing pools and
oversized mushrooms, vanishing into a deep violet glow at the
horizon. The cavern ceiling becomes a "second sky" of glowing
roots, hanging amethyst clusters, and dangling crystal lamps.

This is the **subterranean region** — down where the Realm hums in
its sleep. Bioluminescent, magenta-warm, songs that started
underground and never came up. Reverent, dreamy, and slightly
mystical, but never dim — the warmth holds even underground.

## The path

A soft worn path, dappled lavender-violet, descends from the
bottom-center of the frame in shallow turns. Edges glow with soft
magenta luminescence. The path winds between glowing pools of
phosphorescent water and through clusters of head-tall mushrooms,
disappearing into the violet horizon glow. Where the introduction
uses sunflowers and rolling hills, this scene uses **oversized
glowing mushrooms** (trumpet-shaped, parasol-shaped, tall stalked),
**dangling vine-curtains**, and **clusters of glowing amethyst**
flanking the trail on both sides at head-height.

## Around the path

Past the mushrooms: rolling cavern floor in saturated violet,
magenta, and rose. Pools of phosphorescent water reflect the
glow upward. The cavern walls bend inward at the edges of the
frame, forming a vast natural dome. Tall stalagmites rise like
cathedral pillars in the middle distance, glowing softly at
their tips.

The cavern ceiling takes the upper half — a "second sky" of
**glowing tree-roots** hanging downward, **clusters of amethyst
and quartz** that catch and refract magenta light, and the
silhouettes of dangling crystal lamps. The ceiling glows where
the deepest violet runs into rose-coral pockets — like an aurora
viewed from below. The horizon (deep cavern center) glows a
deep ember-magenta, suggesting heat.

## Psychedelic register

The psychedelia lives in **light, scale, and bioluminescence**,
not surface decoration:

- Mushrooms taller than houses, head-height over the path.
- The cavern roof bends gently downward at the edges; the cave
  breathes.
- Glow blooms around mushroom caps, crystal clusters, and pool
  surfaces like an overexposed photograph.
- Soft melting silhouettes of hanging roots and crystal lamps.
- Multiple small light-sources (phosphorescent pools, glowing
  cap-tips, crystal clusters) at wrong scales.
- The whole scene tilts a few degrees toward dream-logic — scale
  is wrong in a way that feels reverent, not threatening.
- Warm light still wins: even underground, no grey, no desat.
  Wrongness lives in saturation, not darkness.

Heinz Edelmann's *Yellow Submarine* sea-of-monsters and
under-the-wave vistas are the direct lineage. Peter Max's
warm-saturated cosmic-pop palette in magenta-violet register.
Moebius's clean dream-logic horizon. Roger Dean's biomorphic
landscape language (curving cavern walls, oversized organic
forms — take the language, leave the prog-fantasy connotation).

## Composition

- 16:9 aspect (matches the 1280×720 logical viewport at category
  defaults: 400×225 source, scaled up at runtime via Nearest
  filter).
- **Path as leading line.** Foreground bottom-center → midground
  curve between mushrooms → distant violet horizon glow. The
  path starts wide in the foreground and tapers as it descends.
- **Open upper-right corner.** A single floating button will sit
  in the top-right of the runtime UI — leave that area visually
  quiet (cavern ceiling, soft hanging-root silhouettes only).
- 16-bit pixel art register: limited shading bands per surface,
  soft dithering in the gradients, anti-aliased silhouettes,
  single-color outlines on hero shapes (foreground mushrooms,
  path edges, crystal clusters).
- The image is full-bleed, no border, no baked-in lettering.
