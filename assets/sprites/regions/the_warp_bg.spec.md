# The Warp — region background

A yellow-saturated dreamscape where reality has slipped a frame.
A path enters the foreground at bottom-center, curves once, and
then **loops back into itself** — a Möbius-like curl that confounds
where the eye thought it was going. The horizon stutters: a single
line splits into three faint ghost-horizons stacked slightly
offset. Hills melt like Dali clocks. Multiple small suns scatter
across the sky at wrong scales.

This is the **glitch region** — where the Realm forgets which song
it was singing. Color punches sideways, the horizon stutters, and
that's fine. Beautiful and quietly wrong simultaneously. The
warmth is loud; the wrongness is in *geometry and color logic*,
never in dimming.

## The path (looping back)

A wide dirt path enters the frame at bottom-center, looking like
the introduction's path at first glance. Then it curves into
itself — a Möbius-curl, an impossible loop, a road that ends
where it began. The path glows gold-yellow at the edges. Where
the introduction uses head-tall sunflowers, this scene uses
**melting flower-stems** that bend at impossible angles, **floating
flower-heads** detached from their stems and hovering above the
path, and **double-imaged grasses** (each grass clump rendered
twice, slightly offset, as if the world has stuttered).

## Around the path (melted geometry)

Past the path-loop: rolling hills that **melt like Dali clocks** —
hill-tops sag and droop, edges drip downward in slow pastel
streaks. Fields are flat-color in saturated chartreuse and gold,
but **with punch-out hue stripes** running across them: a band of
cornflower-blue where there should be yellow, a band of magenta
where there should be green, a band of coral where there should
be chartreuse. The stripes are wide and obvious — the world's
color logic has slipped.

Sky takes the upper half: a saturated gradient running **gold
into yellow into chartreuse**, but **interrupted twice** by
horizontal hue-shift bands (cornflower, magenta) that don't
belong. **Three small suns** scatter across the sky at wrong
scales — one large and molten on the horizon, one small and
ghost-pale in the upper-right (faint, do not crowd the corner),
one tiny and crimson floating wrong in the upper-left. Soft
melting cloud silhouettes, but **double-imaged** like the
grasses — each cloud rendered twice, slightly offset.

## Psychedelic register

The psychedelia lives in **broken geometry, color punch-outs, and
multiplicity**, not surface decoration:

- The path loops back into itself — impossible road geometry.
- Hills melt and drip in pastel streaks, Dali-style.
- Color punch-out stripes interrupt fields and sky — wide bands
  of the wrong color, cornflower where yellow should be.
- Multiple suns at wrong scales, scattered across the sky.
- Double-imaged grasses and clouds — each rendered twice,
  slightly offset, as if reality has stuttered a frame.
- Floating flower-heads detached from their stems, hovering
  above the path.
- Three faint ghost-horizons stacked slightly offset.
- The whole scene tilts heavily toward dream-logic — wrongness
  is *visible and beautiful*, never dim, never threatening.
- Warm light still wins: gold-yellow dominant. The wrongness is
  in geometry and color logic, not in darkness.

Heinz Edelmann's *Yellow Submarine* most surreal sequences (Sea
of Holes, Sea of Time) are the direct lineage. Peter Max's
warm-saturated cosmic-pop sky in glitch register. Salvador
Dali's melting Persistence-of-Memory geometry — take the
melted-form language, leave the literal pocket-watches. Moebius's
clean dream-logic horizon stuttered into ghost-horizons. Roger
Dean's biomorphic landscape language with the rules broken.

## Composition

- 16:9 aspect (matches the 1280×720 logical viewport at category
  defaults: 400×225 source, scaled up at runtime via Nearest
  filter).
- **Path as leading line — but the line loops back.** Foreground
  bottom-center → midground curl → loops back on itself before
  reaching the horizon. The eye is invited in and then turned
  around.
- **Open upper-right corner.** A single floating button will sit
  in the top-right of the runtime UI — leave that area visually
  quiet (sky, soft glow, the faint ghost-pale sun is small and
  unobtrusive there).
- 16-bit pixel art register: limited shading bands per surface,
  soft dithering in the gradients, anti-aliased silhouettes,
  single-color outlines on hero shapes (foreground melting
  flowers, path edges, the loop curl).
- The image is full-bleed, no border, no baked-in lettering.
