# The Finale — region background

A grand amphitheater carved into a mountainside at red-gold hour.
A stone path climbs from the foreground straight up the mountain
toward a glowing crimson stage at the horizon — a single focal
point where the whole scene converges. The path is the spine of
the composition; every other element arranges itself toward that
distant stage.

This is the **climactic region** — where the Realm sings its last
great chord. Stage that knows your name. Triumphant, hot, vivid,
the moment a journey has been building toward. Solemn but not
grim; the warmth is loud, almost overwhelming.

## The path

A wide stone procession-path, worn smooth, rising in shallow
terraces from the bottom-center of the frame. Edges glow rose and
ember. The path climbs over swelling foothills and disappears
into the crimson glow of the distant stage. Tall crimson poppies
and oversized red tulips line the path on both sides at
head-height — taller than realism allows, leaning toward the
traveler. Where the introduction uses sunflowers and goldenrod,
this scene uses crimson poppies, scarlet roses, and burning-red
amaryllis blooms.

## Around the path

Past the flowers: rolling foothills curving upward into the
mountainside. The mountain itself is a great red-stone amphitheater
shape — concave, embracing, terraced — that holds the stage at its
heart. Towering monolithic standing-stones (or speaker-stack
silhouettes) flank the stage on either side, head-and-shoulders
above the horizon, casting long warm shadows down the slopes.

Sky takes the upper half: a saturated gradient running **rose-coral
into crimson into ember-red**, deepening as it nears the horizon
glow. A faint cornflower band sparingly at the very top of frame
for contrast. Two suns hang low — the larger one a molten
crimson-orange disc resting on the horizon directly behind the
stage, the smaller a pale gold disc nearby. Soft pastel cloud
forms in melting silhouettes.

## Psychedelic register

The psychedelia lives in **scale, light, and convergence**, not
surface decoration:

- Crimson poppies head-tall, leaning over the path.
- The mountain bends gently inward — embracing the stage.
- Glow blooms around the stage, the suns, and the brightest
  flower-tops like an overexposed photograph.
- Two sun-discs overlapping behind the stage, one molten-large.
- Standing-stones flank the stage like a procession of giants.
- Long warm-red shadows reach down the slope toward the viewer.
- The whole scene tilts a few degrees toward dream-logic — scale
  is wrong in a way that feels grand and ceremonial, not
  threatening.

Heinz Edelmann's *Yellow Submarine* climactic vistas are the
direct lineage. Peter Max's saturated cosmic-pop sky in red
register. Moebius's clean dream-logic horizon. Roger Dean's
biomorphic mountain language (curving inward terraces, oversized
stones — take the language, leave the prog-fantasy connotation).

## Composition

- 16:9 aspect (matches the 1280×720 logical viewport at category
  defaults: 400×225 source, scaled up at runtime via Nearest
  filter).
- **Path as leading line.** Foreground bottom-center → midground
  terraced climb → distant stage at horizon center. The path
  starts wide in the foreground and tapers as it climbs.
- **Open upper-right corner.** A single floating button will sit
  in the top-right of the runtime UI — leave that area visually
  quiet (sky, soft glow).
- 16-bit pixel art register: limited shading bands per surface,
  soft dithering in the gradients, anti-aliased silhouettes,
  single-color outlines on hero shapes (foreground poppies, path
  edges, standing-stones).
- The image is full-bleed, no border, no baked-in lettering.
