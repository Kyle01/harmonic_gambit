# HP Potion — item icon

A small **glass bottle of red potion**, classic JRPG inventory icon.

## Shape

Round-bellied bottle with a short narrow neck. Brown cork stopper on top.
Centered in the frame. Bottle takes up roughly 60-70% of the canvas.

## Color

The liquid inside is a bright **crimson / blood-red**. Glass has a faint
cool highlight on the upper-left curve to read as round. Cork is a warm
**brown**. A single white pixel highlight on the bottle reads as a glass
shine.

## Background

Fully **transparent** — no scenery, no ground, no shadow.
