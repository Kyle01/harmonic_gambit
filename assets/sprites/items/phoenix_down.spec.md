# Phoenix Down — item icon

A single **fire-colored feather**, classic Final Fantasy revive item.

## Shape

A long curved feather, quill at the bottom-left, plume sweeping up to the
top-right. Slight S-curve. Centered, fills most of the frame.

## Color

Plume gradient from **deep orange-red** at the base to **bright golden
yellow** at the tip. Quill / shaft is darker red-brown. A few thin
yellow highlights along the spine of the plume to give it warmth.

## Background

Fully **transparent** — no scenery, no ground, no shadow.
